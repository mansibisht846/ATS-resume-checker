Job Title: Data Analyst
Company: TechCorp Solutions
Location: Delhi, India

About the Role:
We are looking for a Data Analyst to join our growing analytics team. You will be responsible for collecting, processing, and analyzing large datasets to help drive business decisions.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze large datasets using Python and SQL to extract meaningful insights
- Build interactive dashboards and reports using Power BI and Tableau
- Perform data cleaning, data wrangling, and exploratory data analysis (EDA)
- Collaborate with business teams to understand data requirements
- Create visualizations using Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Develop and maintain Excel-based reports for stakeholders
- Apply statistical analysis and machine learning techniques to business problems
- Present findings and insights to non-technical stakeholders

Requirements:
- Proficiency in Python including Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn
- Strong SQL skills for data querying and database management
- Experience with Power BI and Tableau for dashboard creation
- Knowledge of data cleaning and exploratory data analysis
- Familiarity with machine learning concepts and scikit-learn
- Strong Excel skills including pivot tables and vlookup
- Good communication and presentation skills
- Experience with GitHub for version control

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with customer behaviour analysis
- Knowledge of retail analytics or business analytics
- Internship experience in data science or analytics
- Familiarity with NLP and text analytics
- Understanding of statistics and probability
